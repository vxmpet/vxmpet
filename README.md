### 🎨 About Me

I'm a **20-year-old Software Engineer** based in Germany, driven by a passion for building elegant, efficient, and lightweight solutions. I love diving deep into how things work—whether it's modern backend systems or the internals of legacy hardware.

- 🛠️ **What I'm doing:** Pushing into the Nintendo DS Homebrew scene.

- ⚙️ **Passion:** Creating tools that empower other developers and gamers to get the most out of their hardware.
